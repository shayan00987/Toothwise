Focus Topic by Dr Osama

Importance of "Doodh Kay Dant" Healthy Baby Teeth, Healthy Future

Many parents think milk teeth are temporary and do not require care. In reality, early loss of baby teeth leads to severe crookedness in permanent teeth, speech issues, and nutritional struggles.

Guides Adult Teeth

They act as space maintainers for permanent teeth to emerge correctly.

Chewing & Nutrition

Cavities cause pain, leading children to avoid wholesome chewing foods.

Speech Clarity

Front teeth play a vital role in proper tongue positioning for speech.

First Visit by Age 1

Catch tooth decay and bottle rot before it requires complex treatment.
